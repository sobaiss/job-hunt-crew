# A filter declares its support per site, and the candidate sees it before the run

A Search filter a site could not express was silently dropped: HelloWork's `SiteConfig.notes` recorded `postedWithin` and `remote` as deliberately unmapped, Adzuna folds `remote` into its keyword query, Remotive ignores `location`, and nothing ever told the candidate. This is not a theoretical risk — five of the seven Scouts in the development database ask for `postedWithin: 14d`, and two of those target HelloWork, so they promise "last 14 days" in the UI and return offers of any age. Each (site, filter) pair therefore carries a FilterSupport — `SUPPORTED`, `APPROXIMATED` or `UNSUPPORTED`, with optional per-value derogations — declared in `py-db` beside the Site adapter that honours it, served by `GET /site-configs`, and shown both in the Scout form and on the saved Scout.

## Consequences

**Three states, not two.** "This filter shifts relevance without excluding anything" is neither honoured nor ignored, and it is the common case rather than the exception: Adzuna injects `remote` as a search term, and LinkedIn's `f_JT` classifies by working time and engagement with no notion of contract duration, so `CDI → F` is a category error rather than a translation. A candidate who asked for telework needs to know which of the two they received. Post-filtering deliberately counts as `SUPPORTED`, not as a fourth state — Remotive's `postedWithin` is applied in this pipeline rather than by the API, and the candidate gets exactly what they asked for; where the filter runs is not their concern.

**Declared in code, not in a `SiteConfig` column.** A seeded column could assert that HelloWork honours `postedWithin` while the adapter ignored it, and nothing would catch the drift — the defect this ADR exists to remove, reintroduced one layer up. `py-db` is the home because `services/api` and `services/ingestion` both already depend on it and on nothing of each other's, so neither a new cross-context dependency nor a duplicated table is needed to have one service declare and another serve.

**Derogations widen, never narrow.** `14d` has no equivalent on HelloWork, which offers a week or a month, so it becomes a month. An offer outside the filter arrives carrying the date that lets the candidate dismiss it in a glance; an offer never returned cannot be noticed at all. This generalises what `api_sources.filter_by_posted_within` already does for Remotive by keeping offers with no `postedAt`.

**A filter no site honours is hidden, not warned about.** Showing a permanent warning on a field that is `UNSUPPORTED` everywhere teaches candidates to ignore warnings, which devalues the ones that genuinely vary from site to site. Such a field is hidden from the form until some site honours it; `experienceLevel` is the only one today, and it keeps its key in the Search filter so that restoring it needs no migration.
